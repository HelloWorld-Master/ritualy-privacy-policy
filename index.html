Ritualy works offline. It does not send your feelings, ritual activity, or settings to us. It saves only your language and theme on your device. Support email and visits to this webpage are handled separately below.

Who is responsible

Ritualy is a Paperplane Craft app published by Dmitrii Fomin in Berlin, Germany. He is responsible for the data handling described here.

Email: paperplanecraft.studio@gmail.com
Postal address: see the Impressum

The app

  • Saved on your device: your language and light or dark theme. The app uses your device language as its initial choice. You can clear these settings by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it; a device backup may retain a separate copy.
  • Used only while the app runs: the feelings you select, the rituals you open or finish, timers, and the haptics switch. The app keeps no history of them.
  • Device features: Android vibration permission is used for optional haptic feedback. The singing-bowl sound is built into the app. It does not use your microphone, camera, or location.

If you download Ritualy from an app store, that store handles the download under its own privacy policy (Apple or Google).

If you email us

If you choose to email us, we receive your address and message and use them to answer you. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to users (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep messages only as long as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations.

Our support mailbox uses Gmail. Google may process messages outside the European Economic Area; see Google’s data transfer information.

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub says it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security; we do not receive those logs. Our legal basis for providing this page is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GitHub Pages’ data notice and GitHub’s privacy statement for details, including transfers outside the European Economic Area.

We add no cookies, analytics, ads, external fonts, or scripts to this page.

Your rights
